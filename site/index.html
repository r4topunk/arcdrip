01 / the problem

Paying a team onchain today means picking a compromise.

Streams know about time but not about teams. Splitters know about teams but not about time. Manual transfers know about neither. ArcDrip is the merge: a split that happens over time.

MANUAL TRANSFERSSomeone signs every period.Miss one and payroll is late. Amounts live in a spreadsheet. Runway is a guess.
ONE STREAM PER PERSONN streams, N balances.Changing one weight means rebuilding every stream. The treasury’s runway is scattered across N deposits.
SPLITTERDivides what arrives, at once.Great for revenue, wrong for salaries: there’s no notion of time, so nothing is earned per second.
ARCDRIPA split that streams.One balance and one runway. Re-weighting is one write. Anyone can press pay, and money always lands at the member’s address.
02 / how it works

The rate is constant.
The slices move.

Read it left to right: the pool’s whole rate is the height of the band, and each color is one member’s slice. Every event below is a single transaction.

1 · setShares(D, 1)Someone joins mid-streamEveryone’s slice shrinks from this second. One write; nobody else’s storage is touched.
2 · balance hits zeroThe pool freezes by itselfNo keeper, no debt. It can never owe more than it holds.
3 · deposit(amount)Anyone tops it upStreaming resumes from the deposit’s timestamp. Frozen time isn’t back-paid.
4 · setShares(C, 0)Someone leavesC stops earning, but everything C already accrued stays withdrawable forever.

03 / the math

Why one write is enough.

The pool keeps a single running number: how much one share has earned since the start. Each member only remembers where that number was when they joined. Their pay is the gap, times their shares.

O(1)Join, leave or re-weight settles one member and touches nobody else.

04 / guarantees

What’s earned is theirs.

An owner can pause, re-weight or cancel the future. Nothing can touch the past. Each rule is a Foundry invariant, checked after every step of randomized runs.

I1 · no over-accountingWhat members can claim never exceeds what streamed.
I2 · solventA pool never owes more than its balance.
I3 · backedAll pools together are covered by the contract’s USDC.
I4 · owner-proofNo owner action ever lowers anyone’s claimable.
I5 · never over-paysChecked against an exact rational reference model.
skip ≠ revertIn “Pay everyone”, a blocked address is skipped and its balance restored. The rest still get paid.
0 admin keysOne immutable contract. No upgrade, no token, no fees. Pool owners can be an EOA, a Safe or a DAO.
